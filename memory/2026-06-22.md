### 2. Roll Labels Pricing Way Too High
- **Symptom:** 3"×3" Standard Matte @ 1K qty showing ~$1.97/label (should be ~$0.29)
- **Root cause:** Two issues:
  1. Whole-roll billing: `Math.ceil(quantity / labelsPerRoll) * rollCost` charged for entire rolls even on small orders
  2. Flat 2.2× markup + tiny volume discounts didn't reflect competitive market pricing
- **Fix applied:**
  - Proportional roll costs: `(quantity / labelsPerRoll) * rollCost`
  - Tiered markups replacing flat 2.2× + volume discounts:
    | Qty | Markup | Margin @ 3×3 Std |
    |-----|--------|------------------|
    | 250 | 152% | ~60% |
    | 500 | 120% | ~55% |
    | 1,000 | 60% | ~38% |
    | 2,500 | 40% | ~29% |
    | 5,000 | 20% | ~17% |
    | 10,000+ | 18% | ~15% |
- **Result:** 3"×3" Standard Matte @ 1K qty now $0.29/label (competitive with StickerMule $0.27)
- **BOPP premium:** ~$0.04/label more than standard paper
- **File:** `src/lib/roll-label-pricing.ts`
- **Commit:** `5077ed7`
