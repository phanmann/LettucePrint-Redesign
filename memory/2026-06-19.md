## LP Website — Roll Labels Pricing Fix (Deployed)
- **Issue:** Calculator was charging for entire material rolls even on small orders, producing absurd prices (~$1.97/label at 1K qty vs StickerMule's $0.27)
- **Root cause:** `Math.ceil(quantity / labelsPerRoll)` + `rollsNeeded * rollCost` billed whole rolls
- **Fix:** Changed to proportional roll costs — `(quantity / labelsPerRoll) * rollCost`
- **New markup tiers (replacing flat 2.2x + volume discounts):**
  | Qty | Markup | Margin @ 3x3 Standard |
  |---|---|---|
  | 250 | 152% | 60.3% |
  | 500 | 120% | 54.5% |
  | 1,000 | 60% | 37.5% |
  | 2,500 | 40% | 28.6% |
  | 5,000 | 20% | 16.7% |
  | 10,000+ | 18% | 15.3% |
- **Result:** 3"×3" Standard Matte @ 1K qty now $0.29/label (was $1.97), competitive with StickerMule ($0.27)
- **BOPP premium:** ~$0.04/label more than standard paper
- **Ink cost:** $0.017/sq in (unchanged)
- **Files changed:** `src/lib/roll-label-pricing.ts`
- **Deployed:** <https://lp-redesign.vercel.app/shop/roll-labels>
- **Commit:** `364f682` — "fix(roll-labels): proportional roll costs + custom markup tiers"
