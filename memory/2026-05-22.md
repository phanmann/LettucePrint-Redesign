## White Rabbit Energy — 3D Can Render Plan
- Steven asked how to convert Drive PNG can mockups (`CB1`, `DR`, `IT`, `MB`) into high-quality spinning 3D renders for a website redesign.
- Findings: best production workflow is Blender with one master 12 oz can scene, flat label artwork wrapped as cylindrical textures, and automated batch rendering for stills/spins/optional GLB. Existing mockup PNGs are useful references but not ideal final textures because shadows, perspective, and highlights are baked in.
